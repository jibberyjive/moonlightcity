## 🌟 1. The Opening Hook

### The Pitch (one paragraph)

**Moonlight City is going dark.** Once, the whole city glowed silver at night — the lamps didn't need fire, the fountains shimmered, and the moon looked *close enough to touch*. That's why they named it Moonlight City. But the glow has been fading for twenty years, one lamp post at a time, and nobody can agree why. On the day you arrive, holding a house key and a letter from an aunt you've never met, only **three** lamps in the whole city are still lit.

### The Letter (opening cinematic text)

The game opens on a boat pulling into **The Docks** at night. The player reads a letter, one line at a time, in a handwriting font:

> *To my grand-niece or grand-nephew — I don't know which one of you is reading this, so hello to both!*
>
> *This is the key to my house in Moonlight City. It's yours now. The roof leaks a little when it rains. Sorry about that.*
>
> *Here's the thing. The city's light is going out, and I don't think it's going out on its own. Something down in the old channels beneath the streets is drinking it. I'm going to go and have a look.*
>
> *If I'm not back by the time you get here — DON'T PANIC. I am extremely difficult to lose. I once spent a week inside a whale and came out with a hat.*
>
> *Three things:*
> *1. Water the plant on the windowsill. She's called Gerald.*
> *2. Don't go into the sewers alone. Make a friend first. Make several.*
> *3. If you find a door with a moon on it — that one's mine. Wait for me.*
>
> *Love always,*
> **— Great-Aunt Wren, the Deepwatch**

### Why the player stays

Three reasons, layered so a young player always has *something* they want:

| Layer | The hook |
|-------|----------|
| 🏠 **Cozy** | You own a slightly wonky little house. It's yours! Decorate it. Get a bigger one. |
| 💛 **Social** | Wren's instruction was literally *"make a friend first."* Friendship is a **quest objective**, not a side activity. |
| 🔍 **Mystery** | Where is Wren? What's eating the moonlight? What's behind the moon door? |

### The Mystery (what's actually going on)

The "sewers" under Moonlight City **aren't sewers.** They're the **Moonwater Channels** — a beautiful old system of glowing pipes and canals that carried moonwater from a deep spring called the **Moon Heart** up into every lamp, fountain, and window box in the city.

Twenty years ago, a single small slime slipped into the channels and discovered that moonwater tastes *amazing*. It drank. It grew. It kept drinking. It has been growing in the dark for twenty years and it now sits on top of the Moon Heart like a cat on a keyboard, absolutely delighted, with no idea whatsoever that a whole city upstairs is going dark because of it.

**The Slime King is not evil. He's just enormous, and hungry, and extremely rude about it.**

That reveal is the emotional payoff of Zone 1 — the "monster" turns out to be a big dumb happy thing that needs to be *moved*, not destroyed. (See [After the Boss](#after-the-boss-the-lights-come-on).)

Every NPC follows the same structure:
**Backstory → 3-Stage Personal Quest → Dungeon Connection → Gifts & Rewards.**

### 🧪 Ella — The Cheerful Shopkeeper

> *"Welcome to Ella's! Everything's fresh, everything's fizzy, and if it isn't, you didn't buy it from me."*

**Personality type:** Friendly · **Home turf:** Downtown Market (day) · Her flat above the shop (night)

#### Backstory

Ella is the third Ella to run **Ella's Marvellous Mixtures**. Her grandmother, **Granny Bell**, founded it, and the family's famous potions all share one secret ingredient: a splash of real **moonwater**, tapped from a little brass pipe in the shop cellar.

The pipe stopped running four years ago. Since then Ella has been quietly cutting her potions with ordinary well water, and they're... fine. They're fine! They're just not *marvellous*. Her bottles have gone cloudy, her healing potions take three sips instead of one, and she has started laughing a little too loudly when customers mention it.

Ella is the most cheerful person in the city and she is holding it together with tape. Her arc is about letting a friend see the cellar.

#### Personal Quest Arc — *"The Fizz Went Out"*

| Stage | Name | Unlocks at | What happens |
|-------|------|-----------|--------------|
| **1** | **Cloudy Bottles** | Acquaintance | Ella asks you to fetch 5 × *Clean Slime Jelly* from floors 1–5 to test a new recipe. It fails hilariously — the potion turns into jam. She laughs. You notice she doesn't stop laughing for a while. |
| **2** | **Granny's Missing Page** | Friend | She admits the moonwater pipe is dry and shows you the cellar. Page 41 of Granny Bell's recipe book is missing — Granny tore it out and took it down into the channels years ago. Find it in a flooded side room on **Floor 12**. It reads: *"If the moon runs dry, don't fake it. Go and unclog it, you daft girl."* |
| **3** | **The Moonwater Tap** | Best Friend *(requires Floor 25 cleared)* | With moonwater flowing again, Ella reopens the cellar tap and runs a stall at the Moon Festival. She names the new drink after the player. It is aggressively fizzy. |

#### Dungeon Connection

Ella is your **supply line**. She is the reason you can afford to go down. Story-wise, her failing shop is the player's first *proof* that the Dimming is real and hurting people — before any big speech from the Mayor.

#### Gifts & Rewards

- **Loves:** Glowcap Mushroom, Clean Slime Jelly, anything shiny, compliments about the shop
- **Dislikes:** Sewer Rat Tails (she shrieks — comedically)
- **Rewards:** 10% → 25% shop discount · free daily potion at Friend · **Delivery Crate** at her house-visit tier (potions delivered to your home chest) · recipe: *Fizzpop Tonic*

### 🗡️ Rex — The Gruff Ex-Adventurer

> *"You want sewer advice? Here it is. Don't. ...Fine. Sit down. Don't touch the lantern."*

**Personality type:** Snarky / gruff-with-a-heart · **Home turf:** The Rusty Bucket tavern, The Slums (evening) · The seawall (early morning)

#### Backstory

Twenty years ago, four people called themselves **the Deepwatch** and went down into the channels to find out why the lights were dimming. Rex was the muscle. **Wren** — the player's great-aunt — was the mapmaker.

They got as far as Floor 17 and found something huge in the dark. Rex's nerve broke, his knee broke shortly after, and he came up. Wren went back down alone, six times over the next twenty years. Rex never went with her.

He has sat at the same table in the Rusty Bucket ever since, with a rusted lantern in front of him that hasn't lit since that night. He tells everyone the sewers are a waste of time. What he means is *I'm scared, and I let my friend go alone.*

He is gruff, not mean. He notices when you're hurt. He always has a spare bandage.

#### Personal Quest Arc — *"One Last Descent"*

| Stage | Name | Unlocks at | What happens |
|-------|------|-----------|--------------|
| **1** | **The Rusty Lantern** | Acquaintance | He'll trade sewer tips for a **Moonglass Shard** (Floor 8). When you bring it, the lantern flickers — one heartbeat of silver light — and he goes very quiet and says *"Huh."* Then changes the subject. |
| **2** | **Half a Map** | Friend | He gives you the left half of the Deepwatch map, torn down the middle. The right half is still at the **Deepwatch Camp on Floor 17** — bedrolls, a cold kettle, and Wren's handwriting on the wall: *"REX — went on ahead. Not your fault. Feed my plant."* |
| **3** | **One Last Descent** | Best Friend *(before Floor 25)* | Rex straps on his old armour, complains about it the entire way, and **fights the Slime King at your side as an escort NPC**. He is not very good anymore. He is trying extremely hard. |

#### Dungeon Connection

Rex is the **living map** of Zone 1 and the emotional spine of the Wren mystery. Each Deepwatch landmark you find (Floors 4, 11, 17, 22) unlocks a new Rex memory scene at the tavern.

#### Gifts & Rewards

- **Loves:** Lamp Oil, Old Coins, Tavern Stew, being asked for advice
- **Dislikes:** Being called old. Flowers (*"What am I supposed to do with these? ...I'll put them in water."*)
- **Rewards:** Technique **Guard's Stand** (block-and-counter) · free tavern healing · sparring partner at the guild · at Best Friend he finally lights the lantern and gives it to you: **Deepwatch Lantern** (+2 vision radius in dark rooms)

### 🎨 Lumi — The Shy Artist

> *"Oh! I— hello. Sorry. I didn't— I'm painting. You can look. If you want. You don't have to."*

**Personality type:** Shy · **Home turf:** Residential Park bench (afternoon) · Her attic studio (night)

#### Backstory

Lumi paints the moon every single night, from the same bench, and has done since she was small. She's the best painter in the city and would rather be swallowed by the ground than say so.

Here's the strange part: for the last two months, Lumi has been **waking up next to paintings she doesn't remember making** — dripping green tunnels, brass pipes, a room full of glowing mushrooms, a shape in the dark with too many eyes. She has never been in the sewers. She has never even been *near* the sewer entrance.

The explanation is gentle and lovely: Lumi is **moon-touched**. Her paints are ground from moonglass (Granny Bell's supplier, Auntie Fen, sells the pigment), and moonwater *remembers places*. When she sleeps, the paint shows her where the moonlight went.

Her arc is about a shy person discovering her strange gift is **useful**, and finding the courage to show it to an entire city.

#### Personal Quest Arc — *"The Paintings That Paint Themselves"*

| Stage | Name | Unlocks at | What happens |
|-------|------|-----------|--------------|
| **1** | **A Place You've Never Been** | Acquaintance | She nervously shows you a painting of a cavern full of glowing mushrooms. You find it on **Floor 6** — identical, down to the crack in the wall. Bring her a Glowcap from it. She sits down very suddenly. |
| **2** | **The Colour That Ran Out** | Friend | Her greens and silvers keep drying grey — the pigment needs live moonwater. Gather **Moonglass Dust** (Floors 10–20) so she can mix a batch that stays bright. In exchange, each new painting becomes a **free map hint** for an undiscovered secret room. |
| **3** | **The Mural** | Best Friend *(Floors 20+)* | Lumi paints a four-storey mural on the Slums wall, in public, in front of everyone, hands shaking. It shows the Moon Heart, the throne room, and the King sitting on it — the clearest picture anyone has ever had of what's down there. It's also just... beautiful. The city stops to watch. |

#### Dungeon Connection

Lumi is the **oracle**. Her paintings are the in-fiction reason the player gets hints, secret-room markers, and boss-arena foreshadowing. Mechanically, each completed stage adds paintings to the player's house that glow when you're near an undiscovered secret on your current floor.

#### Gifts & Rewards

- **Loves:** Glowcap Mushroom, Moonglass Dust, Blank Canvas, quiet company (standing near her without talking raises friendship!)
- **Dislikes:** Crowds, loud surprises, being rushed
- **Rewards:** Free house paintings & wall/floor colour unlocks · **Secret-Sense** hints · she starts leaving small gifts on your doorstep, unsigned

### 🌑 Rose — The Mysterious Figure

> *"You're loud. Even standing still, you're loud. ...Sit. Watch the water. I'll answer three things."*

**Personality type:** Mysterious · **Home turf:** The Docks, **night only** · vanishes at sunrise

#### Backstory

Rose wears a deep hood and speaks in short sentences and everyone in the Slums has a rumour about them. All the rumours are wrong.

Rose is the last **Moonwarden** — the order that once maintained the Moonwater Channels, opening and closing the great brass valves so the city got exactly enough light. The Wardens worked by moonwater, drank moonwater, and slowly *became a little bit made of it*.

That's why Rose only comes out at night. And it's why Rose is **fading** — as the moon dims, so do they. Their hands have gone faintly see-through. The hood isn't hiding something scary. It's hiding something *beautiful*: a face full of drifting silver freckles like a slow snowfall of stars, which Rose thinks makes people stare.

Rose knows exactly what's on Floor 25 — and Rose's position is that the King should be **moved, not hurt**. *"It isn't wicked. It's a mouth. Mouths aren't wicked."*

#### Personal Quest Arc — *"The Last Warden"*

| Stage | Name | Unlocks at | What happens |
|-------|------|-----------|--------------|
| **1** | **Three Questions** | Stranger → Acquaintance | Rose won't take gold, only trades. Bring one **Moonglass Shard** per question. Ask about the sewers, about Wren, about the moon. The answers are true, useful, and infuriatingly short. |
| **2** | **The Warden's Keys** | Friend | Three brass **valve keys** are still in the channels — Floors 10, 15 and 20. Each one you turn re-opens a channel and **relights a district of the city above** (Docks → Slums → Downtown). Rose grows visibly more solid with each one. |
| **3** | **Unhooded** | Best Friend | At the Moon Festival, Rose lowers the hood in front of the whole city. There's a long silence. Then Pipper says *"WHOA, COOL"* and everyone agrees. Rose gives you the **Moonwarden's Lantern** and — for the first time — smiles. |

#### Dungeon Connection

Rose is the **lore-keeper and the moral compass**. They provide: the truth that the sewers are channels, the valve-key objectives that link floor progress to visible city change, and the plan for the boss fight that isn't "hit it until it stops."

#### Gifts & Rewards

- **Loves:** Moonglass Shard, Deep Pearl, Silver Key, being left alone in a companionable way
- **Dislikes:** Prying questions before Friend level, torches shoved in their face
- **Rewards:** Free channel fast-travel between valve rooms · **Moonwarden's Lantern** (reveals hidden doors) · night-market access · Rose's true name, which they only tell a Best Friend: **Sable**

### 🎩 Mayor Aldric — The Pompous Mayor

> *"Moonlight City has never been brighter! Metaphorically. Which is the important kind of brighter."*

**Personality type:** Pompous (secretly anxious) · **Home turf:** City Hall (day) · balcony rehearsing speeches (evening)

#### Backstory

Aldric inherited the mayor's chain from his father and has been terrified of dropping it ever since. He is not corrupt. He is not a villain. He is a nervous man who has decided that the way to handle a city slowly going dark is to **paint the lamp posts more often** and **hold bigger festivals**.

Twenty years ago, when the Deepwatch went down and only three came back, Aldric — brand new in the job, hands shaking — signed the order that **sealed the sewer entrance** and declared the channels "decommissioned." He told himself he was protecting people. He mostly was. He also made sure nobody could go looking for the one who stayed down there.

Rex has never forgiven him. Aldric has never forgiven himself. He keeps the signed order in his desk and takes it out more often than he'd admit.

His arc is the simplest and the best one for a kid: **it is never too late to admit you got it wrong.**

#### Personal Quest Arc — *"The Speech"*

| Stage | Name | Unlocks at | What happens |
|-------|------|-----------|--------------|
| **1** | **A Ribbon For Everything** | Stranger → Acquaintance | Pure comedy. Hold the ribbon. Polish the statue (of himself). Retrieve his hat from the fountain. Attend the grand opening of a bench. He pays *very* well and thanks you in the third person. |
| **2** | **The Sealed Order** | Friend | Behind the sealed gate on **Floor 1** you find the original decree with his signature. You can hand it to him — or quietly burn it. **Handing it over is the better outcome**: he sits down on the City Hall steps, in public, and tells you the truth. |
| **3** | **The Speech** | Best Friend *(after Floor 20)* | At the Moon Festival, Aldric climbs onto the stage without notes and tells the whole city that the lights are going out, that he knew, and that people are down there fixing it right now. Then he unseals the **Old Deepwatch Hall** and funds the expedition. Rex, in the crowd, gives one small nod. |

#### Dungeon Connection

Aldric is the **gatekeeper**. He's why the sewer entrance is barred at the start of the game, and his arc is what unlocks the Deepwatch Hall — the guild hub used for Zone 2 onward.

#### Gifts & Rewards

- **Loves:** Fine Hats, Polished Medals, Rare Documents, being *genuinely* complimented (he can tell the difference and it wrecks him)
- **Dislikes:** Slime (on anything), being laughed at
- **Rewards:** Big gold payouts · **Housing Permit** (unlocks Tier 3 & 4 homes) · city title cosmetics · **Key to the Old Deepwatch Hall**

## 🗺️ 3. Main Story Arc — *The Dimming* (Floors 1–25)

### The Golden Rule

> **Every floor milestone in the dungeon changes something the player can SEE in the city.**

This is the whole design. The dungeon isn't a separate mode — it's how you fix your town. Going deeper literally turns the lights back on.

### The Moonlight Meter 🌕

A city-wide meter shown on the map screen, from **0% (only 3 lamps lit)** to **100% (the city glows silver)**.

| Meter | What changes in the city |
|-------|--------------------------|
| 0–20% | Dark streets. Shops close early. NPCs stay indoors. Ella's potions are cloudy. |
| 21–40% | The Docks relight. Night fishing opens. Rose becomes more solid. |
| 41–60% | The Slums relight. Black Market moves into a real shop front. Rex starts standing up. |
| 61–80% | Downtown relights. Fountains shimmer. Festival preparations begin. Housing Tier 3 unlocked. |
| 81–100% | Full glow. Night becomes a *second daytime* with its own NPC schedules and events. |

The meter rises from **valve keys** (Rose), **cleared floor milestones**, and **completed friendships** — city life and dungeon feeding the same bar.

---

### 🎬 Prologue — *"Welcome to the Dark"* (No dungeon)

**Beats:**
1. Arrive at the Docks at night. Read Wren's letter. Meet **Rose** on the pier for eight seconds before they vanish.
2. Find the house. It's dusty. Gerald the plant is *alive but furious.* Water Gerald — first quest complete.
3. Morning. Meet **Ella** (buys your first potion), **Pipper** (sells you a crayon map), **Mayor Aldric** (makes you hold a ribbon).
4. Go to the sewer entrance in the Slums. It's **sealed** — Aldric's decree nailed to the grate.
5. **Rex** is watching from the tavern doorway. *"That grate's been shut twenty years. ...Wren, was it? Yeah. I knew her."*

**Prologue ends when:** Rex tells you where the *other* way in is — a cracked storm drain behind the Rusty Bucket.

---

### 🌊 Act I — *"Something's Clogging the Pipes"* (Floors 1–8)

**Tone:** Adventure! Splashing! Small slimes! Everything is a bit gross and very exciting.

| Floor | Story beat |
|-------|-----------|
| **1** | Behind the sealed gate: Aldric's original decree *(Mayor Quest 2 item)* and a very startled **small slime you can befriend — Bubbles.** |
| **3** | Bubbles follows you home and lives in a jar on your shelf. Teaches the player that slimes aren't monsters, they're *animals*. |
| **4** | **Deepwatch Landmark #1** — a chalk arrow and four sets of initials: R · W · A · F. *(Rex, Wren, Aldric, Fen — all four were down here.)* |
| **6** | The **Glowing Mushroom Cavern** from Lumi's painting. Proof her gift is real. |
| **8** | The walls stop being brick and start being **brass pipe.** The first working **moonwater trickle** — it's beautiful, and it's silver, and it's flowing the *wrong way*: down, not up. |

**Act I question:** *Why is the moonlight going down instead of up?*
**City change:** Docks relight. Moonlight Meter → 25%.

---

### 🔦 Act II — *"The Deepwatch Trail"* (Floors 9–17)

**Tone:** Mystery and history. You are walking in your great-aunt's footsteps.

| Floor | Story beat |
|-------|-----------|
| **10** | **Valve Key #1.** Turn the wheel. Cut to the surface: a whole street of lamps flickers on and people run outside to look. |
| **12** | Granny Bell's missing **Page 41** *(Ella Quest 2)*, in a flooded reading nook with a teacup still on the shelf. |
| **15** | **Valve Key #2.** Also: the first **Toxic Slimes**, and the first hint of scale — a footprint. Not a footprint. A *sit*print. Twelve metres across. |
| **17** | ⭐ **THE DEEPWATCH CAMP.** Four bedrolls. A cold kettle. Rex's half-map completes here. On the wall, in chalk, in Wren's hand: *"Rex — went on ahead. Not your fault. Feed my plant."* |

**Act II ends with** the "Rex Memory" scene at the tavern where he finally tells the player what happened twenty years ago — the one quiet, serious scene in the game. He does not cry. His voice does a thing.

**Act II question:** *Where did Wren go, and what did they all run from?*
**City change:** The Slums relight. Moonlight Meter → 50%.

---

### 🍮 Act III — *"The Feast Below"* (Floors 18–24)

**Tone:** Big. Building. A little bit awed. The comedy and the scale both go up.

| Floor | Story beat |
|-------|-----------|
| **18** | The tunnels get *wider* and *cleaner* — because something enormous has been squeezing through them, polishing the walls. |
| **20** | **Valve Key #3** — but the wheel is jammed with jelly. Downtown relights only *partially*. Something is pulling harder than you are. |
| **21** | **Giant Slime mini-boss.** It splits. Then those split. Pipper's crayon map says "EASY :)". Pipper was wrong. |
| **22** | **Deepwatch Landmark #4** — Wren's pack, her boots, her journal. **No Wren.** The last entry: *"Found the Heart. Found the King. He's not cruel — he's just BIG and he's SITTING ON IT. Can't move him alone. Going round to the moon door. Back soon."* |
| **23** | The **Moon Door** — silver, carved with a crescent, sealed, warm to the touch. **It does not open.** Not this zone. *(Zone 2 hook.)* |
| **24** | The **Hall of Lanterns** — a thousand dead Moonwarden lamps. Rose meets you here, fully solid for the first time, and says: *"Don't kill it. Wake it up. There's a difference."* |

**Meanwhile, on the surface:** the **Moon Festival** happens and **the lanterns won't light.** The city stands in the dark square in silence. Then Aldric gives **The Speech**, Lumi unveils **The Mural**, and Ella hands out fizzy drinks to a crowd that badly needed one. It's the warmest scene in the game — and it happens at the darkest moment. The whole city then walks you to the sewer entrance.

**City change:** Moonlight Meter caps at 60% and *will not rise further* until Floor 25.

### 👑 Floor 25 — The Slime King

> *"From the deepest filth of the sewers, a consciousness has formed — ancient, patient, and absolutely delighted with itself."*

**The arena:** the **Moon Heart Chamber** — a vast round cavern with a silver spring at the centre... entirely covered by a slime the size of a house, wearing a crown of fused brass valve-wheels and old crockery it has swallowed. It is **glowing from the inside** with all the light it has drunk. Honestly? It's kind of magnificent.

**Escort NPCs (if befriended):** Rex fights beside you. Rose shields you from slime waves. Both are optional — and both make the fight noticeably easier *and* funnier (Rex has barks for every phase).

| Phase | HP | Behaviour | Story flavour |
|-------|-----|-----------|---------------|
| **1** | 100–60% | Stomps, throws slime balls, spawns small slimes | It thinks you're a snack. It is not taking this seriously. |
| **2** | 60–30% | Grows, gains a charge attack, slimes turn Toxic | It finally *notices* you. The glow inside it flares. |
| **3** | 30–0% | Berserk. Floor floods with slime. Rapid slams. | It's not angry — it's **scared**. It doesn't want to be moved. Rex: *"It's holding on! Don't stop!"* |

**The finish is not a killing blow.** At 0% HP the King doesn't burst — it **deflates**, releasing twenty years of stored moonlight in one enormous silver flood that rushes up every pipe in the city at once.

### After the Boss — *"The Lights Come On"*

1. The moonwater surges upward. **The entire city relights in a single wave**, district by district, in a long silent camera pan from the Slums to the Docks. Moonlight Meter → 100%.
2. Left in the middle of the chamber: a **very small, very confused, apologetic green slime**, wearing a crown three sizes too big. Rose: *"Told you. It was just a mouth."*
3. You may pick him up. He is now **Prince Gloop**, your house pet. He follows you. He eats furniture if you don't feed him. He is Bubbles' best friend immediately.
4. Loot: big gold, rare chest, **👑 The Slime Crown** (wearable cosmetic — a dripping green crown that *actually drips*, cosmetically, forever), and the **Key to Zone 2**.
5. **The stairs down are now visible** behind the Moon Heart — the Underground Crypts.
6. 📜 **THE SECOND LETTER**, pinned to the throne where the King was sitting, in Wren's handwriting, dated **eleven days ago**:

> *Well done. I knew whoever read my letter would be the sort to actually turn up.*
>
> *I couldn't move him. You could. That's the whole difference between us and I'm not remotely bitter about it.*
>
> *I've gone through the moon door. It only opens from the Crypt side, which is a design flaw I intend to complain about to somebody.*
>
> *Come and find me. Bring Rex. Tell him I said the knee excuse expired years ago.*
>
> *— Wren*
>
> *P.S. If a small slime has adopted you, feed him. Not furniture. He'll tell you furniture. He's lying.*

7. **Homecoming scene:** you climb out of the storm drain into a Moonlight City that is *silver*. Every NPC you befriended is in the square. Ella's stall is running. Lumi's mural is glowing because the pigment finally has live moonwater. Rex lights his lantern. Rose lowers the hood. Aldric hands you the Deepwatch Hall key. Pipper demands you sign her map.
8. **New systems unlock:** Old Deepwatch Hall (guild), night city (a whole second set of NPC schedules), Tier 3–4 housing, and the descent to Floors 26–50.

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 4. Three New NPCs

Chosen to fill three gaps in the current cast: **a kid the player's age**, **a warm caretaker**, and **a friendly rival**.

---

### 🎒 Pipper — The Junior Deepwatch Captain (age 10)

> *"I'm not ALLOWED in the sewers. But you are! So technically if you go, I've been. That's how it works. I checked."*

**Personality:** Excitable · **Location:** Downtown fountain & the bulletin board · **Why she matters:** She's the same age as the player playing beside a parent, and she's the one who thinks *you* are the coolest person alive.

- **Backstory:** Pipper appointed herself Captain of the Deepwatch (membership: one). She sells **crayon maps** of the sewers for 5 gold. They are roughly 60% accurate, which is *unsettlingly* good for someone who has never been down there — because she gets her details from listening to Rex talk in the tavern doorway.
- **Runs:** the **Bulletin Board** — daily bounties, community tasks, "wanted" posters she drew herself.
- **Quest arc:** (1) *Map Correction Service* — mark 5 real landmarks so she can fix her maps. (2) *Captain's Badge* — she wants a real one; Rex has one in a drawer and needs persuading. (3) *The Junior Deepwatch* — she recruits the city's kids to relight and maintain the lamps, and gives you the honorary rank of **First Mate** (she outranks you and mentions it constantly).
- **Rewards:** free daily bounty · a genuinely useful map upgrade · a growing wall of her drawings *of you* in your house.

### 🌿 Auntie Fen — The Herbalist of the Slums

> *"Sit. Show me. Mm. That'll sting. — There. Now: have you eaten today? Don't lie to me, I can see your hands shaking."*

**Personality:** Warm, blunt, motherly · **Location:** The little green door clinic, The Slums (always open) · **Why she matters:** the safety net. There is always one person in the world who will patch you up for free and ask if you've had lunch.

- **Backstory:** Fen was the **fourth Deepwatch** — the healer. She was the one who got Rex out on a bad knee, and she's the one who supplies Lumi's moonglass pigment. She's the only member who never blamed anyone for anything, which is why she's the only one all four still speak to.
- She keeps a list on her wall of **"everyone who owes me a bandage"** — a running joke that turns lovely when you learn Wren's name is on it, uncrossed, for twenty years.
- **Quest arc:** (1) *Bandages and Bad Habits* — gather herbs; she teaches you to make Healing Salve. (2) *The Long List* — help her deliver free medicine across the Slums at night; you learn six NPCs' small quiet stories. (3) *Crossing a Name Off* — after Floor 25, she finally crosses Wren's name off... then writes it again, at the top of a fresh list, because *"she's not done owing me yet."*
- **Rewards:** free healing forever · **Healing Salve** crafting · +max HP herbal meals · she checks on you at home if you faint in the dungeon.

### ⚔️ Will — The Rival (age 15)

> *"Floor twelve. Four minutes, nine seconds. — Oh, hi! Didn't see you there. What was YOUR time? Say it louder, I'm writing it down."*

**Personality:** Snarky, competitive, secretly a total softie · **Location:** Guild Hall, sewer entrance, or wherever you are (annoyingly) · **Why she matters:** friendly rivalry = replay motivation, and a great character for a kid to beat.

- **Backstory:** Will is a Blade Dancer who has been trying to be the youngest person to clear Floor 25 since she was twelve, and she has failed **nineteen times**, because she always goes alone. She has Rex's poster on her wall and would rather eat the poster than admit it.
- **Mechanic:** every floor has a **ghost time**. Beat Will's, and she posts a new one, with commentary. Lose, and she leaves you a healing potion "out of pity" that is definitely just kindness with a hat on.
- **Quest arc:** (1) *Rival's Challenge* — beat her time on Floor 5. (2) *Twenty Times Unlucky* — you find her at the Floor 18 checkpoint, out of potions, out of pride, and have to **team up** to get out; the game's first co-op-flavoured floor. (3) *Two Names on the Board* — she stops racing you and asks to come with you, and the Floor 25 leaderboard gets **two** names on it.
- **Rewards:** Blade Dancer techniques · speedrun cosmetics · escort ally · and at Best Friend, the single most awkward thank-you speech in the entire game.

**Bonus companions:** 🫧 **Bubbles** (Floor 1 rescue slime, lives in a jar, reacts to everything) and 👑 **Prince Gloop** (post-boss pet, eats furniture, is sorry).

## ✍️ 6. Writing Rules for All Dialogue

For anyone adding NPC lines later — keep the tone consistent:

1. **No true villains.** Every antagonist is a misunderstanding, a fear, or a very hungry animal. The Slime King is the thesis statement.
2. **Nobody dies.** Ever. People get lost, scared, tired, or stubborn. They come back.
3. **Kindness is a mechanic.** Asking *"are you okay?"* should always be a valid dialogue option, and it should always give the biggest heart gain.
4. **Every NPC gets one joke and one truth.** The joke earns the trust; the truth pays it off.
5. **Short bubbles.** Max ~3 lines per bubble. If it's longer, split it — a child is reading this out loud.
6. **Gruff ≠ mean.** Rex is never cruel to the player. Not once. He's short, not unkind.
7. **Give the player good options.** Every choice should be something a kind kid would actually want to say. No "be a jerk" branch.
8. **Silliness is allowed at any depth.** Gerald the plant. The emotional support pickle jar. Prince Gloop eating furniture. The tension is *earned* by the warmth, not the other way round.
